Nepal Premier League (NPL) 2026 Tournament Overview

Sanctioned by the Cricket Association of Nepal (CAN), the NPL features Nepal's finest domestic cricketers alongside renowned international superstars competing across 32 thrilling T20 matches at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur, Kathmandu.

Which teams participate in the Nepal Premier League?

Eight franchise teams compete: Pokhara Avengers, Sudurpaschim Royals, Kathmandu Gurkhas, Janakpur Bolts, Biratnagar Kings, Karnali Yaks, Chitwan Rhinos, and Lumbini Lions.
